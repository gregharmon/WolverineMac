OpenJoystickDriver
https://github.com/xsyetopz/OpenJoystickDriver
Source commit: d43199315ce713cc8c5f543077cf5da616b61efb

VirtualGamepad.mm adapts the Xbox Series Bluetooth HID descriptor from
Sources/OpenJoystickDriverKit/Output/HID/Xbox/Bluetooth.swift. The matching
identity was checked against VirtualDeviceProfile.swift from the same commit.
The C++ Xbox 360 input encoder and IOKit publisher are local implementations.
The authoritative 17-byte report layout uses physical Xbox Series Bluetooth
button masks, hat, axes, and triggers from SDL's official HIDAPI Xbox One parser:
https://github.com/libsdl-org/SDL/blob/075e1d87596ca5595da181233d72e1cea4942239/src/joystick/hidapi/SDL_hidapi_xboxone.c
OJD's older contiguous button packer is not used.
